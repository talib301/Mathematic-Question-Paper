Q1. Solve any two parts:

(a) By using the properties of determinant, prove that

\[ \begin{vmatrix} x & y & z\\ x^2 & y^2 & z^2\\ y+z & z+x & x+y \end{vmatrix} = (x+y+z)(x-y)(y-z)(z-x) \]

(b) If

\[ A = \begin{bmatrix} -9 & 1 & 2\\ 5 & 1 & 4\\ 2 & 8 & -6 \end{bmatrix} \]

find the value of \(A^2-3A+5I\), where \(I\) is a unit matrix of order 3.

(c) Find the inverse of matrix \(A\)

\[ A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 3\\ 2 & 1 & -1\\ 4 & -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \]

Q2. Solve any two parts:

(a) Express the complex number

\[ \frac{2+i\sqrt{3}}{5+i\sqrt{3}} \]

into polar form.

(b) If \(n\) is a positive integer, prove that

\[ (\sqrt{3}+i)^n+(\sqrt{3}-i)^n = 2^{n+1}\cos\left(\frac{n\pi}{6}\right) \]

(c) Solve the differential equation

\[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} -6\frac{dy}{dx}+9y=e^{-2x} \]

(b) Find the Laplace transform of the function

\[ f(t)=4\sin^3 4t+2\sin 2t\sin 3t \]

\[ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} -4\frac{dy}{dx}+4y=64\sin 2t \]
